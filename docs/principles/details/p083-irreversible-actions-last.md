# P083 — Irreversible Actions Last

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- The author groups steps as read-only, reversible, compensatable, and irreversible.
- The author completes validation and reversible preparation before any irreversible step.
- The author uses previews, staging, backups, canaries, and dry runs where they fit.
- The author binds approval and authorization to the publication target and parameters.
- The author re-examines mutable facts immediately before the irreversible operation.

## Limits

No output is rejected because compliance cannot be shown. Credential and PII protection still applies.
