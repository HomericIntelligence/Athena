# P009 — General Mechanisms Over Special Cases

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- Conditions that share one rule are handled by one mechanism.
- Variation that does not change the rule is shown in data or a contract.
- Tables and normalized representations are preferred over scattered branches.
- The shared invariant is named before an abstraction is made.
- Special cases for a genuinely different requirement are kept.
- A clear explicit branch can beat a general engine with hidden policy.

## Limits

No result is rejected because compliance cannot be shown.
