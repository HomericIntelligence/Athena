# P072 — Technical Evidence Over Preference

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- The author states the disputed claim and the observation that can distinguish the alternatives.
- The author compares evidence for relevance, trustworthiness, reproducibility, and applicability.
- The author prefers requirements, specifications, tests, and measurements over opinion.
- The author uses representative data when the environment changes behavior.
- When evidence is missing or equal, the author records the uncertainty and prefers convention or owner preference.

## Limits

No output is rejected because compliance cannot be shown. Credential and PII protection still applies.
