# P006 — Principle of Least Astonishment

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- Behavior agrees with what the intended audience expects.
- Names, defaults, units, and errors match related conventions.
- Destructive or surprising behavior is clear, confirmed, or clearly named.
- Related commands and interfaces behave the same way.
- When a difference from precedent is needed, the difference is made clear.
- Defaults and failure behavior are part of the public contract.

## Limits

No result is rejected because compliance cannot be shown.
