# P002 — YAGNI

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- Work focuses on the current requirement, not on future guesses.
- No extension points, flags, or configuration exist only for a future that may never come.
- Clear contracts and tests keep future change possible without building it now.
- Ideas for future work are recorded with an owner or trigger, not built.
- When real needs appear, the design grows to meet them.
- Explicit quality requirements and specified migrations stay in scope.

## Limits

No result is rejected because compliance cannot be shown.
