# P086 — Readability Counts

This principle is a goal for engineering work. It does not authorize or block an action.

## What it looks like

- The author selects the design that the reader can examine with the least effort.
- The author uses domain names that give role and units.
- The author gives each function or module one behavior.
- The author prefers linear control flow and named intermediate results over compressed expressions.
- The author makes invariants and failure branches easy to find.

## Limits

No output is rejected because compliance cannot be shown. Credential and PII protection still applies.
